06 — Guidelines
Do's & Don'ts
✓ Do
Use the full-color logo on white or light backgrounds
The primary logo with Stratum Blue icon on white creates maximum clarity and brand recognition.
✕ Don't
Stretch, rotate, or distort the logo
Never alter the proportions of the logo mark or wordmark. Never apply drop shadows or outlines to the icon.
✓ Do
Maintain adequate clear space around the logo
Always keep a buffer of at least one "s" height around the entire logo on all sides.
✕ Don't
Place the blue logo on a blue background
Never place the primary logo on Stratum Blue. Use the reversed white version on colored or dark backgrounds.
✓ Do
Use DM Mono for specs and measurements
Product dimensions, tolerances, and part IDs should always use DM Mono for a precise, engineering feel.
✕ Don't
Introduce additional typefaces
Stick to DM Sans + DM Mono only. Never introduce third-party fonts that dilute typographic consistency.
